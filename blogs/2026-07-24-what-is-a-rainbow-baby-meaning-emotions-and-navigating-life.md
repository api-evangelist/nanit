If this term found you after a loss, you might be holding a lot of emotions at once: a rush of hope, a wave of grief, or likely a quiet mix of both. The term “rainbow baby” refers to a child born after a miscarriage, stillbirth, or infant loss; symbolizing hope after a storm without erasing the grief that came before it. Navigating such a life experience isn’t about "moving on" but finding balance, carrying forward both the love for the child you lost and the joy for the new life arriving.
