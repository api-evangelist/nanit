If you still find comfort in checking the screen to make sure your preschooler is sleeping safely, welcome to the club. Using your baby monitor for older kids is not unusual, as it’s often a practical way to balance their safety with their growing need for independence—especially during that period from 18 months through early school age. That said, as your child grows, the way you use that technology should evolve with them.
