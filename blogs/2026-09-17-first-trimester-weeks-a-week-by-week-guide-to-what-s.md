Seeing that positive test can bring a mix of excitement, joy, and—let's be honest—a heavy dose of anxiety. Suddenly, you might find yourself Googling every twitch and twinge, wondering if what you're feeling is "normal." If you feel pressure to master pregnancy overnight, take a deep breath; you don't need to know everything right now. This guide is designed to help simplify your first trimester weeks (weeks 1–12), offering a clear view of what is developing inside, why some things feel the way they do, and a useful checklist.
