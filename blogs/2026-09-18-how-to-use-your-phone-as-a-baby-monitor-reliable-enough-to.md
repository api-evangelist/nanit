We've all been there: settling your baby down for a nap in an unfamiliar room and realizing you need a watchful eye. Knowing exactly how to use your phone as a baby monitor is the ultimate parenting quick fix for these situations—whether you are traveling, visiting family, or just waiting for a new camera to arrive in the mail. Using your phone works incredibly well as a temporary or backup monitor, especially when you can pair two devices to create a parent station and a camera in the baby's room.
